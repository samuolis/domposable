# Fieldwork storefront assets

Original demo artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool. Unbranded fictional products; bundled locally for offline native demos and deterministic web captures. Source files live in `demo/src/commonMain/composeResources/files/`.

## shop-hero

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: original landscape hero photograph for a familiar ecommerce storefront demo called Fieldwork. Scene: quiet sunlit studio with warm cream plaster, oak tabletop, soft morning shadows. Subject: a moss-green canvas weekender bag, natural oatmeal linen throw, cream ceramic mug and a small sculptural terracotta lamp arranged as a refined still life. Style: realistic editorial product photography, tactile fabric and ceramic texture, restrained natural colors. Composition: wide 1536 by 1024 landscape, products occupy center and right with open breathing room, all products fully inside frame. Constraints: original unbranded products, no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI.
